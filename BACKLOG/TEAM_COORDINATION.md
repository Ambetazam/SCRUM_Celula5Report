# 👥 Individual Contribution and Team Coordination Record

## Purpose

This section records the individual initiative undertaken during the development of the project when responsibilities, review activities, and coordination requirements were not sufficiently defined within the team.

The purpose is not to assign personal blame, but to establish an objective record of the activities undertaken and the communications used to coordinate the work.

## Situation Observed

During the development process, communication was maintained through the team group chat. Requests were made regarding the review of previously produced documentation, the continuation of the assigned work, and the coordination of pending activities.

In several instances, the responses received did not establish a specific task, responsible person, deadline, or subsequent action. One recurring response was essentially a general confirmation such as:

> “listo agale”

Because this type of response did not establish a sufficiently detailed task assignment or acceptance criterion, additional initiative was required to prevent the corresponding project activities from remaining undocumented or incomplete.

## Initiative Taken

To reduce the risk of receiving an incomplete evaluation due to undocumented individual work, the undersigned proceeded to:

* Review the existing project documentation.
* Identify pending documentation and organizational needs.
* Propose and prepare user histories applicable to the corresponding project cell.
* Review the technological stack and documentation structure.
* Organize evidence of participation and communication.
* Identify bibliographic and legal references requiring inclusion.
* Maintain communication with the team regarding pending work.
* Preserve available evidence of the coordination process.

## Evidence

The following evidence may be attached to this record:

1. Group-chat screenshots.
2. Git commits and branches.
3. Pull requests.
4. Document revision history.
5. Previous versions of the requirements document.
6. User histories prepared for the assigned cell.
7. Messages requesting review or coordination.
8. Contributions to the final project documentation.

## Statement

The purpose of documenting these activities is to ensure that individual contributions remain traceable regardless of the degree of coordination achieved by the group.

The undersigned recognizes that the project is collaborative and that successful completion requires participation from all members. For that reason, the evidence is presented objectively so that the instructor or evaluator can independently determine the contribution of each participant.

This record does not seek to replace the responsibilities of the Scrum Master or other team members. Its purpose is to demonstrate that, when specific coordination or task assignment was not provided, independent work was undertaken to maintain progress and reduce the risk of an incomplete project submission.
